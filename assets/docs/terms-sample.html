HSB Loyalty — Terms & Conditions

Version 1.0 · Effective 1 October 2026

1. About the programme

HSB Loyalty rewards clients with coins for trading. Coins can be redeemed for gifts and vouchers in the rewards store.

2. Who can take part

  1. Clients with a verified account who have made a first deposit.
  2. Only eligible trading accounts earn coins. Some account groups are excluded.

3. Earning coins

  1. Coins are earned per lot traded. The rate depends on the symbol group.
  2. A position must be held for the minimum time to earn coins.
  3. There is a maximum number of coins that can be earned per day.
  4. Coins are credited to the balance after the crediting period.

4. Expiry

Coins expire a fixed number of days after they are credited. Expired coins cannot be restored.

5. Redeeming

  1. Coins that expire soonest are used first.
  2. Rewards are subject to availability and may change without notice.

6. Misuse

The company may withhold or remove coins earned through abusive trading, and may suspend participation.

7. Changes

The company may change or end the programme at any time. The latest version of this document applies.
