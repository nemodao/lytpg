HSB Loyalty

Three screens of the loyalty programme, built to run in the app’s webview at 375px wide. All figures are sample data.

Tab Home Dashboard Coin balance, the Daily Trading task and rewards. Open Tab Earn Daily Trading Today’s progress, trading account and earning rates, how coins are earned, history. Open Tab Coins Coin Balance Available coins, expired coins and coin history. Open

How to review

  • Open it on a phone for the closest match to the app. On a computer, the screen shows centred at its 375px width.
  • The “States” button at the bottom right switches situations on and off, for example a zero balance, the daily coin limit reached, or the programme ended. States are grouped by the part of the screen they change and can be combined. On a computer, hover over a state to read what it does.
  • Light and dark follow your device setting. To switch, open “States” and pick a Theme.
  • The bottom bar moves between the three screens. Buttons that would leave these pages (Redeem, Trade, KYC, Deposit, the X) show a short “Internal Explanation” of where they lead instead.

Offline copy: unzip the whole folder, then open index.html. No internet needed. The “States” button and the explanations are review aids only, not part of the product.
